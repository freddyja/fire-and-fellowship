Fire and Fellowship — Household & Headship

A single-page web app for the Fire and Fellowship men's group. Draw a random Scripture (KJV) or prayer on a man's calling to lead, love, provide, and father well — each one paired with a discussion or prayer prompt for the group. Available in English, Spanish, and Portuguese. Includes a printable full handout for meetings.

No build step, no dependencies, no server. It's one HTML file.

Features
Draw a Verse — pulls a random entry from 25 verses + 5 original prayers, filterable by topic: Leadership, Love & Sacrifice, Provision, Fatherhood, Prayer
Full Handout — every verse and prayer on one page, grouped by topic, with a "Print / Save as PDF" button formatted for paper
EN / ES / PT — full interface, verse, and prayer translation, switchable at any time
