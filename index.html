This Week's Assignment

Week 1

Ask a brother: "What's one thing you wish I knew about you?" Then only listen. Don't fix, don't explain, don't defend. Just hear him. Grace starts with attention.

For the Group

Discussion Questions

  1. I

    Where in your life are you still trying to earn God's approval instead of living from it?

  2. II

    How does knowing salvation is a gift change how you handle your failures?

  3. III

    What would it look like this week to live from grace rather than toward it?

  4. IV

    Who is a man you consider a model of grace? What specifically does he do?

  5. V

    If your brothers were asked what kind of man you are, what would you like it to be?
